# ROADMAP v6 — Clean the base, then analytics, then surveys and ratings

**You are done with v6 when** two identical callbacks race and produce one bill; a forced Prisma error leaks no contact anywhere; `TAX_COMPLIANT` renders at document width with a component-ladder tax summary and a real invoice date; an inter-state bill has been looked at in a browser; every PG order carries a sale time parsed from its raw callback string and interpreted in IST, with unparseable ones counted as unattributed rather than silently dropped; a merchant opens a dashboard and sees revenue by source, volume, average value, tax breakdown and busiest hours — every figure grouped by currency, every bucket in IST; that merchant builds a survey and a rating, attaches each to a template, issues a bill, and a customer answers it from the public page without logging in; editing the survey afterwards leaves the issued bill byte-identical; an anonymous survey's table contains no bill reference anywhere; and **every v1, v2, v3, v4 and v5 test is still green**.

**Task-ID convention.** `S-` continues as the schema prefix and `X-` as the cross-phase regression prefix. New workstreams take unused letters: `Q-` for quality (Stage A's repairs), `N-` for analytics, `Y-` for surveys, `O-` for ratings (opinion capture, deliberately its own prefix per D-91's separation). `A-`, `B-`, `C-`, `E-`, `F-`, `G-`, `H-`, `I-`, `L-`, `M-`, `P-`, `R-`, `T-`, `U-`, `V-`, `W-` are all in use across v1–v5 and are not reused.

**Stage gates.** A stage does not begin until the previous one's tasks are all verified. The gates are real: Stage C adds the first unauthenticated write endpoint in the system, and it lands on a base with no known defects or not at all.
